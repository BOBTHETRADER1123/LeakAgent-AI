LeakLogic AI 4.8 — Pipeline Diagnostic Card

- Added a Pipeline diagnostic card to the Leakage detection workspace.
- Displays selected measurement inlet flow, outlet flow, gross flow difference, and indicative gross volume imbalance over a user-selected duration.
- Shows branch-level status, residual after entered storage/tap adjustments, meter uncertainty, screening z-score, and stable/full-pipe eligibility.
- Adds a downloadable JSON diagnostic for the selected measurement.
- Explicitly labels the volume as an estimate if the gross inlet/outlet difference were sustained; it is not automatically attributed to leakage.
- Converts invalid spatial CSV content into an HTTP 400 validation response instead of an unhandled HTTP 500.
